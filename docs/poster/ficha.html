Ghosted lines

1 page asked3 drawnWarm-up eligibleLast: Tuesday
Plan the line, ghost it, draw it once.

The exercise that teaches the one habit everything else depends on: decide the line first, then draw it once, from the shoulder, without slowing down to aim.

  1. Mark two dots. Decide where the line starts and ends before the pen touches the page.
  2. Ghost it. Move the pen along the path three or four times, hovering just above the paper.
  3. Draw once. One confident stroke at the same speed as the ghosting. Do not slow down near the end.
  4. Leave it. A wobble or a miss is information. Never go back over a line.
Watch for. Wobbly lines mean you drew too slowly. Curved lines mean the wrist took over. Overshooting the dot is fine for now.
